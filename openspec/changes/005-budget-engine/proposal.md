## Why

Complete the simplified envelope budget and goal behavior defined by the
budgeting baseline.

## What Changes

Activate category rollover, cash/bank and credit-card overspending semantics,
simplified card payments, native-currency boundaries, and goal targets/status.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- None.

### Modified Capabilities

- `specs/budgeting/spec.md`

## Impact

Depends on ledger core and transfers; no full YNAB credit-card automation and
no silent currency conversion.
